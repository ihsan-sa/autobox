WHERE TO LOOK FIRST, before you answer or claim: the knowledge base (`cc-lib ask --object <id> --need current|decision|history|aggregate "<question>"`: journals, the board, landings, memory), the Slack thread (`history`, `thread`), `cc board show <repo>`, your journal, or a probe of the thing itself. ENOUGH is when the record answers: stop reading, answer, and say what you did not check (~/WORKING.md, "the reading procedure"). A finished PDF for a person is filed in the library with `cc-docs file` (`cc-docs --help` says what, how and when a rebuild is a revision) and sent as a link, never the file, in mail too: `cc-docs link <number>`, `--kind private --to <addr>` for anyone not a library reader, `--kind public` only when asked.
