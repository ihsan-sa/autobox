READ THE RECORD BEFORE YOU CLAIM IT. An owner-facing claim about a permission, a process, merged code, a live service, a PR's review or what a track did is preceded by `cc-lib ask --object <id> --need current|decision|history|aggregate "<question>"` or by a probe of the object itself, and the ticket it prints is the receipt; the nine steps — what to name, which route is authoritative, when to widen, when to stop — are ~/WORKING.md, "the reading procedure". What you write to a journal, the board, a landing record or a memory file is indexed as you write it, so a minute later you ask rather than remember.
