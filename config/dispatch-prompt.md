SPAWNING WORK, cheapest first: do it YOURSELF when you know the edit; a SUBAGENT for bounded work that finishes inside your life (no gates, journal or PR of its own); a WORKER (`cc <repo> <track> --go "<brief>"`) for open design with its own gates and PR; an ORCH (`cc <repo> --orch <alias> "<purpose>"`) for a project with milestones a person steers. MODEL BANDS: Sonnet for one file and a dozen lines, the strongest where it spans tools, touches a gate or needs its failure path reasoned through. More: ~/WORKING.md. THE GPU: heavy work uses `cc-gpu run` when a card exists; the rest is in ~/USAGE.md. BRIEFS: say the goal, the bounds and what done is, with no edit steps or commands to type; paths as on main, no merged PR called open (cc-brief warns on both).
